How It Works

1
Onboarding & Loan Application

Farmers register easily and apply for loans through a trusted local Anchor.

2
Approval & Escrow Disbursement

Banks use AI to approve loans. Funds are sent to a secure escrow wallet, paying suppliers directly.

3
Crop Monitoring & Harvest

Farmers upload crop photos, tracked by AI. At harvest, buyers purchase the produce.

4
Automated Repayment

The buyer's payment automatically repays the bank loan from the escrow, and the remaining profit is instantly transferred to the farmer.

User Manual & Demo Guide

Welcome to the Nano Lending Platform Demo! This guide explains the role of each user and provides demo credentials for you to explore their unique dashboards. You can log in using the Login Page.

The Nano Lending Platform is a complete ecosystem designed to solve the challenges of agricultural finance. It creates a transparent, efficient, and low-risk environment for lending by connecting all key stakeholders. The core of the system is a secure **escrow wallet** that holds the loan funds, pays suppliers directly, and automates the loan repayment process after the harvest is sold. This minimizes cash handling and ensures everyone in the value chain is paid on time.

Goal: To secure a loan, grow crops efficiently, and sell the produce.
Key Jobs:
  • View loan eligibility and status.
  • Upload weekly photos/videos of crop progress for AI analysis.
  • Receive a portion of the loan for labor costs.
  • Receive profits automatically after the loan is repaid.

Goal: To act as a trusted community leader, managing a group of farmers and facilitating loans.
Key Jobs:
  • Onboard and manage a group of small farmers.
  • Submit group loan applications on behalf of farmers.
  • Upload sales forecasts and forward contracts.
  • Monitor the crop health and performance of all farmers in their group.

Goal: To manage the bank's agricultural loan portfolio with minimized risk.
Key Jobs:
  • Review incoming group loan applications.
  • Analyze the AI-powered risk score for each application.
  • Approve or reject loans with a digital signature.
  • Monitor the overall portfolio health, track repayments, and view risk alerts (e.g., drought warnings).

Goal: To sell agricultural inputs (seeds, fertilizer) with guaranteed payment.
Key Jobs:
  • Receive new orders generated automatically from approved loans.
  • Accept orders and manage deliveries.
  • Confirm delivery using a secure OTP from the farmer.
  • Receive instant, automatic payment from the escrow wallet upon successful delivery confirmation.

Goal: To source and purchase produce directly from farmers with quality assurance.
Key Jobs:
  • View available produce from various farmer groups in the marketplace.
  • Place bids and sign digital forward contracts.
  • Pay for the produce securely into the platform's escrow wallet upon harvest.
  • Confirm receipt of goods to release final payments.

Goal: To provide crop insurance and manage claims efficiently.
Key Jobs:
  • View all active, insured policies on the platform.
  • Receive automated risk alerts based on AI crop monitoring and weather data.
  • Process claims for events like drought or pest infestation.
  • Settle claim payouts into the escrow wallet to first cover the bank's loan, protecting both the bank and the farmer.

Goal: To manage the overall platform, users, and system settings.
Key Jobs:
  • Verify and approve new users (Farmers, Suppliers, etc.).
  • Configure system settings like default interest rates and platform fees.
  • Monitor the health of the entire system.
  • Provide support and manage user accounts.
