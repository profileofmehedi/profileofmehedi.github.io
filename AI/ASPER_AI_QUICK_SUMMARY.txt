ASPER AI - QUICK MODULE SUMMARY

1. Credit Score
   - Calculates 300-850 credit score and approval status.
   - Input: Income, credit limit, balance, account age, late payments, inquiries.
   - Output: Score, Rating (e.g. "Good"), and Approval Decision.

2. Fraud Analysis
   - Detects suspicious transactions in real-time.
   - Input: Amount, merchant type, location, user home, average spend, tx velocity.
   - Output: Risk score (0-100) and action (Approved/Blocked).

3. Churn Risk
   - Predicts if a customer will leave the bank.
   - Input: Last login days, products owned, complaints, balance trend.
   - Output: Churn probability and retention recommendation.

4. Cash Flow
   - Predicts end-of-month balance.
   - Input: Balance, avg income/expenses, upcoming bills.
   - Output: Projected EOM balance and shortfall warnings.

5. KYC (Status: NOT DONE)
   - Verifies ID documents and detects tampering.
   - Input: ID/Passport type, image data (base64), user name.
   - Output: Verification status and tampering check result.
