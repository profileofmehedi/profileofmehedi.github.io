Introduction
Ultra Smart Command Center Concept

This software serves as the Ultra Smart Command Center for the BEPZA Chairman.


It consolidates data from all EPZs into a single pane of glass, allowing for real-time monitoring of status, investments, exports, work progress, risks, and compliance.

Core Purpose

To enable Data-Based, Quick, and Effective decision-making for the Chairman.


  • Instant Visibility
  • Predictive Insights
  • Centralized Control
High-Level Objectives

Speed

Drastically reduce time required for high-level decision making.

One-glance snapshot of the entire BEPZA ecosystem operation.

Risk Mitigation

Identify urgent issues and red flags before they escalate.
