Board Portal User Manual

Welcome!

This manual guides you through the Board Portal prototype. The system is designed to streamline the entire lifecycle of corporate board meetings, ensuring efficiency, security, and proper governance.

The Meeting Lifecycle Workflow

The application is built around the three core phases of a formal meeting:

Phase 1: Pre-Meeting Preparation
  1. Schedule Meeting: The Secretary/Admin uses the Schedule Meeting page to set the date, time, and participants.
  2. Create Agenda: On the Agenda Management page, the Secretary/Admin builds the meeting agenda, attaches relevant documents, and crucially, marks items as "Decision Required" to prepare them for a vote.
  3. Compile & Distribute Board Pack: Using the Board Pack Creation page, the Secretary/Admin bundles the agenda and all documents into a secure package and distributes the link to members.
  4. Member Review: The Board Member logs in, sees the upcoming meeting, and uses the Document Repository to review all materials beforehand.
Phase 2: During the Live Meeting
  1. Join the Session: All users navigate to the Live Meeting page, the central hub for the session.
  2. Present & Discuss: The Secretary/Admin uses the "Next" and "Previous" buttons to guide the meeting through the agenda items. All users can use the professional chat feature to discuss topics.
  3. Launch a Vote: When a "Decision Required" item is reached, the presenter clicks the "Open Voting" button. This action makes the poll live on the Voting & Resolutions page for all members.
  4. Assign Tasks: During discussion, the presenter can click "Assign Action Item" to create a new task that will immediately appear on the Action Tracking page.
Phase 3: Post-Meeting Follow-up
  1. Draft Minutes: The Secretary/Admin goes to the Minutes of Meeting page, where they can load a template, finalize the official record, and export it as a PDF or Word document.
  2. Track Action Items: All users can visit the Action Tracking page. Members see and update their own tasks, while Admins/Secretaries can monitor the progress of all items.
  3. Analyze & Report: The Administrator can use the Analytics & Reports page to view high-level data on attendance, task completion, and voting patterns for governance oversight.
