# Project: ISLAMIC KNOWLEDGE & DISCOVERY PLATFORM

Act as a world-class UI/UX designer, senior frontend engineer, Islamic knowledge researcher, and creative web experience architect.

Your task is to design and develop a complete, modern, beautiful, interactive, responsive Islamic Knowledge & Question-Answer website using ONLY:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

Do NOT use React, Angular, Vue, Next.js, Bootstrap, Tailwind CSS, jQuery, or any backend framework.

The website must be frontend-only, with realistic demo content and local JavaScript data. Structure the code so that a backend and AI-powered Islamic Q&A engine can be integrated in the future.

## 1. Core Vision

Build an Islamic knowledge platform where people can explore Islam, ask difficult questions, understand Quranic teachings, learn Hadith, investigate common misconceptions, and find well-researched answers to questions that make them confused.

The platform should encourage knowledge, critical thinking, respectful discussion, and understanding.

The experience should feel like a combination of:
- A modern knowledge platform
- An Islamic digital library
- An intelligent question-answer portal
- An interactive Quran and Hadith explorer
- An educational discovery platform

Do not make it look like a traditional religious blog.

2. Understanding the Question
3. Quranic Evidence
4. Authentic Hadith Evidence

6. Scholarly Opinions
7. Logical Reasoning
8. Different Perspectives (where applicable)
9. Common Misunderstandings
10. Conclusion
11. References & Sources

Important:
Never invent Quranic verses, Hadith, references, scholarly quotations, or historical claims.

Clearly distinguish:
- Direct Quranic text
- Hadith
- Tafsir interpretation
- Scholarly opinion
- Historical information
- Contemporary scientific findings
- Personal reasoning

When scholars have different opinions, present the recognized positions fairly and explain the evidence behind them.

Never present a disputed interpretation as unanimous Islamic consensus.

## 19. Content Integrity & Islamic Research Standards

This is the most important requirement.

The website must prioritize authenticity over quantity.

Use recognized primary and secondary sources, including:

- The Quran.
- Sahih al-Bukhari.
- Sahih Muslim.
- Other recognized Hadith collections.
- Tafsir Ibn Kathir.
- Tafsir al-Tabari.
- Tafsir al-Qurtubi.
- Tafsir al-Jalalayn.
- Reliable scholarly publications.
- Recognized Islamic academic institutions.

For every religious claim:
- Include a source.
- Include Surah and Ayah numbers when applicable.
- Include Hadith collection and reference number when available.
- Mention scholarly disagreement where relevant.
- Distinguish established facts from interpretations.
- Avoid fabricated citations.

Do not automatically assume every online religious statement is authentic.

Never use misleading scientific claims to prove religious beliefs.
