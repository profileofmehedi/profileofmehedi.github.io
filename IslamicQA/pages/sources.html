Academic Integrity Standards

Sources & Research Methodology

Authenticity over quantity. Every religious statement, historical claim, and translation is held to rigorous academic verification.

Primary Canonical Tier 1

The Holy Quran (Al-Qur'an al-Karim)

The direct, immutable Word of God revealed in the Arabic language to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) via Archangel Jibril. All Arabic verses are transcribed with full vowel marks (tashkeel). Translations cited rely on globally recognized scholarly standards:

  • English: Saheeh International, translated by Aminah Assami, Amatullah Bantley, and Mary Kennedy.
  • Bengali: Mawlana Mohiuddin Khan (Khadim al-Haramayn edition) & Islamic Foundation Bangladesh.
Primary Canonical Tier 2

The Kutub al-Sittah (The Six Books of Hadith)

Hadiths are sourced directly from the authenticated canonical compilations. For every single Hadith presented on DEENQUEST:

  • Sahih al-Bukhari (Imam Muhammad ibn Isma'il al-Bukhari)
  • Sahih Muslim (Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj)
  • Sunan Abu Dawood (Imam Abu Dawood as-Sijistani)
  • Jami' at-Tirmidhi (Imam Abu Isa at-Tirmidhi)
  • Sunan an-Nasa'i (Imam Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb an-Nasa'i)
  • Sunan Ibn Majah (Imam Ibn Majah al-Qazwini)
Authenticity Grading Policy: We explicitly distinguish between Sahih (Sound/Authentic), Hasan (Good/Acceptable), and Da'eef (Weak). We never attribute fabricated (Mawdu') narrations to the Messenger of Allah.
Secondary Exegesis Tier 3

Classical Tafsir Authorities

Exegesis and context of revelation (Asbab al-Nuzul) are referenced from classical masters recognized across Sunni Islam:

  • Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim by Imam Ibn Kathir (d. 774 AH / 1373 CE)
  • Jami' al-Bayan 'an Ta'wil Ay al-Qur'an by Imam Muhammad ibn Jarir al-Tabari (d. 310 AH / 923 CE)
  • Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an by Imam Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Qurtubi (d. 671 AH / 1273 CE)

Epistemological Categorization Rules

To ensure total intellectual honesty, all content on DEENQUEST strictly demarcates:

Direct Revelation: Categorical verses (Qat'i al-Dalalah) and mass-transmitted traditions (Mutawatir).
Scholarly Ijtihad: Interpretative legal reasoning where valid diversity of opinion (Ikhtilaf) is respected.
Historical Record: Documented historical events distinct from theological dogma.
Scientific Hypotheses: Modern empirical observations clearly separated from immutable scriptural truth.
