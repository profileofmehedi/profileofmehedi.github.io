01 — The Problem

The Paradigm Shift in Healthcare

🔴
Fragmented Legacy Systems
Disconnected modules force staff to re-enter data manually, breeding errors and wasting 3–4 hours per clinician daily on redundant workflows.
💸
Hidden Revenue Leakage
Unbilled consultations, untracked consumables, and shadow pharmacy dispensing silently drain 10–15% of gross revenue every month.
🔒
Critical Data Silos
Lab, Radiology, Pharmacy, and Finance operate in isolation — making real-time decisions impossible and audit trails non-existent.
Moving from Reactive Management to Proactive Intelligence — a unified ERP ecosystem that transforms clinical, financial, and operational data into a single "Source of Truth."
