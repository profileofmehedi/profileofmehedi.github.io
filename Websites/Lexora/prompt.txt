Build a premium law firm/lawyer professional website called **Lexora**.

## Objective

Create a sophisticated website for an independent lawyer or boutique law firm.

The design should communicate:

* Authority
* Trust
* Experience
* Professionalism
* Confidentiality

## Pages

* Home
* About
* Practice Areas
* Attorneys
* Case Experience
* Articles
* Testimonials
* Consultation
* Contact

## Home

Hero:

* “Experienced Legal Counsel You Can Trust”
* Short professional statement
* “Request Consultation”
* “Explore Practice Areas”

Add:

* Years of experience
* Cases handled
* Practice areas
* Client satisfaction

## Practice Areas

Create cards for:

* Corporate Law
* Business Law
* Civil Litigation
* Family Law
* Property Law
* Employment Law
* Contract Law

Each should include:

* Icon
* Description
* Learn More

## Attorney Profile

Include:

* Photo
* Name
* Position
* Biography
* Education
* Bar admissions
* Areas of practice
* Professional memberships

## Case Experience

Do NOT present fake cases as real cases.

Label content as:
“Representative Experience — Demo Content”

Display:

* Matter category
* Challenge
* Approach
* Outcome

## Consultation

Create a consultation request form:

* Name
* Email
* Phone
* Legal area
* Preferred consultation date
* Message

Include strong disclaimer:
“This demo form does not create an attorney-client relationship.”

## Articles

Professional legal insights/blog layout.

## Design

* Elegant
* Editorial typography
* Premium corporate aesthetic
* Dark navy/charcoal + neutral palette
* Subtle gold-like accent if appropriate
* Minimal animations
* Excellent whitespace

## Technical

* Fully responsive
* Reusable components
* SEO optimized
* Accessible
* Proper form validation
* Clean architecture
* No lorem ipsum

The final result must look suitable for a premium lawyer or boutique law firm.
