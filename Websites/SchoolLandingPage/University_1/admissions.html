How To Apply

    Submit Online Application

    Fill out our comprehensive online application form and pay the application fee.

    Send your official high school or college transcripts along with standardized test scores.

    Interview (If required)

    Some specific programs might require an in-person or virtual interview with the faculty.

  • 04.

    Receive Decision

    Wait for your acceptance letter! We generally notify students within 4-6 weeks of applying.
